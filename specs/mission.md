# Mission

**What we build:** A voice AI receptionist for small businesses (clinics, salons, restaurants).

**Why:** Small businesses miss calls while busy. Every missed call is a lost booking. This bot answers every call, books appointments, and routes complex cases to a human.

**Who it serves:**
- **Callers** — anonymous, no accounts. They call to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments, or ask FAQs (hours, prices, location, policies).
- **Business staff** — a few people who review handoffs, check logs, and manage the FAQ knowledge base from a simple dashboard.

**Core promises:**
1. The bot answers every call — no busy signals, no "please hold."
2. It books, reschedules, and cancels appointments through a calendar API.
3. It answers FAQs using RAG over the business's knowledge base.
4. It confirms every booking by reading back name, date, and time.
5. It hands off to a human for complaints, urgent/sensitive requests, or anything it's unsure about.

**Success looks like:** A caller can complete a booking in under 2 minutes, and a business can review all calls and handoffs from the same day in their dashboard.
